About Shukla Chaat House

Located at Shop No. 1, Church Building, 11 Shahnajaf Road in Hazratganj, Shukla Chaat House is one of Lucknow's most loved chaat destinations. What began in 1968 as a small family shop has become a household name for generations of chaat lovers.

The preparations are known for their rich flavour, with traditional methods and freshly prepared spice mixes used daily. Evenings here are busy for a reason — regulars and visitors alike queue up for a plate served hot from the tava.
